Ask your Claude

If you use the AIOS, just say “make me my AIOS avatar”. Anywhere else, paste this into Claude:

Make me an AIOS avatar. Read https://www.the-aios.com/avatar-seeds/manifest.json and download https://www.the-aios.com/avatar-seeds/generator.py (it needs pymupdf and pillow). Ask me one question: a hat for what I do, or my initial? For a hat, pick the closest one in the manifest; if none fits, draw a new hat following the rules in the generator's docstring and render it with --hat-svg until it passes the circle check. For an initial, use my first initial and let me pick a colour from the palette. Show me the result, save the PNG to my Downloads, and tell me where to upload it in Discord.

Upload it in Discord: User Settings → Edit Profiles → avatar → Upload Image. Files: manifest.json · generator.py
